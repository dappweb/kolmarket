# KOLMarket on Arc - Review Deck

Prepared: 2026-07-30

## 1. Positioning

KOLMarket turns creator growth into stablecoin-native campaign infrastructure.

Arc fit:

- USDC-native rails for creator campaign budgets,
- EVM-compatible creator-token and campaign infrastructure,
- predictable campaign accounting,
- programmable-money settlement workflows,
- future Circle/CCTP-aligned capital movement.

## 2. Problem

Creator growth campaigns are fragmented across manual budget handling, offchain KOL tracking, spreadsheet-based rewards, and disconnected token launch activity.

This creates:

- weak settlement evidence,
- poor attribution visibility,
- slow campaign operations,
- unclear boundaries between configured, claimable, and settled reward states,
- limited stablecoin-native tooling for creator growth teams.

## 3. Product

KOLMarket combines:

- Creator Launch workflows,
- KOL attribution and campaign tasks,
- Growth Pool reward state,
- KOLSwap market infrastructure,
- portfolio and reward visibility,
- evidence boundaries for claimable and settled states.
