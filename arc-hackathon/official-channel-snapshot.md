# Arc Official Channel Snapshot

Updated: 2026-07-30

## Purpose

Record current official/public-channel evidence used for KOLMarket Arc outreach planning.

## Arc Website

- Arc official website positions Arc as a stablecoin-native Layer-1 blockchain for stablecoins, tokenized assets, economic contracts, and onchain markets.
- Source: https://www.arc.io/

## Arc Ecosystem

- Arc ecosystem page is a public directory for businesses, infrastructure providers, and organizations building on Arc.
- Current public page text includes directory/filter behavior, but no reliable public project intake URL has been confirmed from the page alone.
- Source: https://www.arc.io/ecosystem

## Arc Community / Arc House

- Arc community page directs builders to Arc House.
- Arc House is the official community hub for builder discussion, events, and ecosystem participation.
- Source: https://www.arc.io/community
- Source: https://www.arc.io/blog/introducing-arc-house-and-the-architects-program

## Circle Developer Grants

- Circle Developer Grants page states that grants support developers integrating USDC payments into their apps.
- Circle says it prioritizes teams with shipping ability, clear technical ownership, traction, pilots, partnerships, revenue, or a credible path to usage/revenue.
- Source: https://www.circle.com/grant

## Arc / Circle Grants Post

- Arc House public grant post points Arc/Circle builders to Circle Grants and Circle Developer Platform review.
- Source: https://community.arc.io/public/blogs/circle-developer-grants-program-relaunches-2026-05-14

## Programmable Money Hackathon

- Encode public programme listings show Programmable Money Hackathon as an active 2026 Arc-related opportunity.
- Public X posts from Arc/Encode also reference the Arc x Encode Programmable Money Hackathon.
- Source: https://www.encodeclub.com/
- Source: https://x.com/encodeclub/status/2077767357108482138

## KOLMarket Current Fit

KOLMarket should keep using this official-channel positioning:

```text
KOLMarket is building USDC-native creator growth markets and campaign settlement infrastructure on Arc.
```

Best matching official themes:

- Stablecoin-native applications
- USDC payment/reward workflows
- Programmable money
- Creator/KOL attribution
- Growth Pool settlement preparation
- Arc/Circle developer platform integration path

## Evidence Boundary

This snapshot supports outreach planning only. It does not prove Arc official review, listing, repost, spotlight, Circle grant submission, Circle grant acceptance, Encode checkpoint submission, or Encode final submission.
