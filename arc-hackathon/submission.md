# KOLMarket Arc Hackathon Submission

Prepared: 2026-07-30

## Project

KOLMarket

## Short Description

KOLMarket is building USDC-native creator growth markets and campaign settlement infrastructure on Arc.

## Long Description

KOLMarket helps teams launch creator growth campaigns, attribute KOL participation, manage Growth Pool reward state, and connect creator-token market infrastructure to stablecoin-native settlement workflows.

The Arc pilot focuses on aUSDC-denominated campaign budgets, creator and KOL attribution, reward-state visibility, and creator-token launch/market infrastructure on Arc Testnet.

## Why Arc

Arc is aligned with KOLMarket because creator growth budgets and campaign payouts are naturally stablecoin-denominated. Arc's USDC-native environment, EVM compatibility, and programmable-money positioning make it a strong venue for auditable creator growth finance.

KOLMarket can help show Arc as infrastructure for more than trading: campaign budgets, KOL attribution, creator reward workflows, and future agent-assisted creator-market operations.
