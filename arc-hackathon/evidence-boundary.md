## Evidence Standards

Encode checkpoint evidence requires:

- visible platform success state or receipt,
- timestamp,
- screenshot,
- status/stage after submit,
- browser URL or platform confirmation.

Fresh Arc transaction evidence requires:

- Arc Testnet transaction hash,
- successful explorer status,
- matching wallet,
- matching contract/action,
- product flow alignment,
- timestamp and screenshot.

Official Arc attention requires:

- Arc official reply, repost, quote, spotlight page/post, ecosystem listing, or direct official confirmation.

## Operating Rule

Public materials can be shared for review. Official status claims require direct evidence from the relevant official channel, platform receipt, or explorer-confirmed transaction.
