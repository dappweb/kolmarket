# KOLMarket on Arc

Prepared: 2026-07-30

## Summary

KOLMarket is building USDC-native creator growth markets and campaign settlement infrastructure on Arc.

The Arc pilot focuses on aUSDC-denominated campaign budgets, creator/KOL attribution workflows, Growth Pool reward state, and creator-token market infrastructure. The goal is to help teams fund, verify, and prepare settlement for creator growth campaigns with stablecoin-native rails.

## Why This Matters

Creator growth campaigns often rely on fragmented offchain workflows:

- campaign budgets managed outside the product,
- KOL work tracked manually,
- rewards calculated in spreadsheets,
- creator-token launches separated from growth activity,
- and limited evidence for settlement state.

KOLMarket aims to bring these workflows into a more auditable and stablecoin-native model.

## Why Arc

Arc is a strong fit for creator growth finance because campaign budgets and creator rewards are naturally stablecoin-denominated.

KOLMarket is using Arc Testnet to explore:

- predictable campaign operations with USDC-denominated gas,
- aUSDC-denominated Growth Pool workflows,
- transparent reward-state boundaries,
- creator and KOL attribution,
- EVM-compatible launch and market infrastructure,
- and future Circle/CCTP-aligned crosschain campaign capital flows.
